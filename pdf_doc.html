📌 1. Problem Statement & Solution Overview

Problem: University campus-la damaged furniture (broken chairs/desks), faulty electrical equipment (sparking switchboards, non-working fans), water leakages (washrooms, labs), and classroom AV issues informal-ha report aaguthu. Athanaala repair status track panna mudiyama pending-la poguthu.

FixIt Solution: Oru centralized web platform. Students, Teachers, and Admin ellarume issues-ai photo, location, priority-yoda submit panni, real-time-la resolution progress monitor panna mudiyum.

🌟 2. Full Prototype Feature Breakdown (Each Feature Explained)

1. Submit Complaint Portal (Photo + Room Details)

Core Feature

Students & staff campus-la irukkum issues-ai detail-ha report panna intha modal use aaguthu.

  • Category Picker: Electrical, Plumbing, Furniture, Classroom AV, HVAC/AC, Civil, Sanitation என 7 categories.
  • Exact Location Mapping: Building Select (Main Block, Science Lab, Quad, Library), Floor level, and exact Room/Hall number.
  • Priority Level: Low (72h), Medium (24h), High (12h), Critical (4h SLA).
  • Evidence Photo Upload: Camera மூலமாகவோ அல்லது drag & drop மூலமாகவோ photo attach பண்ணி preview பார்க்கலாம்.

2. ✨ AI Smart Triage Assistant (Auto-Prediction)

AI Innovation

User complaint description type pannum bothe (e.g. "Switchboard sparking with burning smell"), built-in AI NLP engine text-ai analyze pannum.

  • Auto-detects Category (e.g. ⚡ Electrical) & Priority (🚨 Critical).
  • One-Click "Apply Suggestion" click panninaal, form values automatic-ha select aagidum.

3. 🎙️ Voice-to-Text Dictation (Speech Recognition)

Smart Tool

Type panna kastama irunthaal, 🎙️ "Voice Dictation" button-ai click panni pesinaal, Web Speech API ungal kuralai text description-ha convert pannidum.

4. 🗺️ Interactive 2D Campus Map & Zone Heatmap

Visual Map

Campus-oda full bird's eye view 2D map:

  • 🔴 Red Dot / Heat: Critical emergency issues உள்ள labs/blocks.
  • 🟡 Amber Dot: In-progress repair active-ha irukkum buildings.
  • 🟢 Green Dot: All facilities clear & normal.
  • Building click panninaal, antha building-oda active complaints mattum filter aagi kaatum.

5. 📱 Room QR Code Scanner & Door Sticker Generator

QR Automation

Room doors-la irukkum QR Code-ai scan panninaal, Building & Room no instant-ha auto-fill aagidum. Admin-ku printable QR stickers generate panni print edukkum facility irukku.

6. 👍 "Affected Me Too" (+1 Upvote & Duplicate Prevention)

Crowdsourcing

Orey hall-la broken projector-ku 50 students thani thani complaint poda thevailla. Irukkum complaint-ku "Affected Me Too (+1)" click panninaal, severity score automatic-ha increase aagum.

7. ⏳ SLA Countdown Timers & Auto-Escalation

SLA Engine

Every ticket-kum live countdown timer run aagum (Critical: 4h, High: 12h, Medium: 24h). Time expire aanaal "🚨 SLA Breached - Escalated" nu alert aagum.

8. 📋 Kanban Resolution Workflow Board

Agile Board

Operational 3-column stage pipeline: Pending Verification → In Progress & Assigned → Resolved & Closed. Single click-la technician tickets-ai advance panna mudiyum.

9. 🎯 Live Ticket Tracker (by Unique ID)

Tracker

Student FIX-2026-001 nu enter panninaal, 3-Stage Visual Stepper, Assigned Technician Name, Contact, and Complete Update Trail theriyum.

10. 📸 Before vs After Repair Comparison & ⭐ 5-Star Rating

Quality Assurance

Repair mudintha udan Before Photo vs After Photo comparison theriyum. Student antha velaiyin tharathukku ⭐ 1 to 5 Star Rating & Feedback kodukkalam.

11. 💬 Live SMS / WhatsApp Alert Simulator

Live Alerts

Ticket register aanaalum, status marinaalum student-oda phone-ku pogum SMS / WhatsApp live alert preview card modal.

12. 🌱 Green Campus Sustainability & Eco-Impact Score

Eco Metric

Plumbing repairs moolam save aana Water (Litres), Electrical repairs moolam save aana Power (kWh), and University Eco Score (96.4/100) real-time-la calculate aagum.

13. 💾 Data Storage (LocalStorage) & CSV Export

Persistence

Ella complaints-um browser-oda localStorage-la save aagidum. Page refresh panninaalum data irukkum. "Export CSV" click panni Excel file-ha download pannalam.
